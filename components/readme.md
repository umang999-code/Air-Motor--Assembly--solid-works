# 🔩 01 – Components

## 📌 Overview

This folder contains the individual SOLIDWORKS components used to
create the complete Air Motor Assembly.

Each component was independently modelled in SOLIDWORKS before being
integrated into the final assembly.

---

# 🧱 Base Plate:

The base plate is the foundation of the assembly.

It supports the brackets and other structural components.


# 🏗️ Support Bracket:

The support bracket provides structural support to the cylinder
and rotating mechanism.


# 🔵 Cylinder:

The cylinder forms the working chamber for the piston.

The piston moves inside the cylinder due to compressed-air pressure.



# 🔄 Flywheel:

The flywheel is connected to the crankshaft.

It stores rotational energy and helps maintain smoother rotation.



# ⚙️ Crankshaft:

The crankshaft is the main rotating member.

It receives motion from the crank and transfers the rotary motion
to the flywheel/output.



# 🔗 Connecting Rod:

The connecting rod provides the connection between the piston and
crank.

It transfers the piston movement to the crank mechanism.



# 🟢 Piston:

The piston moves linearly inside the cylinder.

Compressed air acts on the piston surface and produces the force
required to drive the mechanism.



# ⚙️ Crank pin:

The crank is attached to the crankshaft.

It transforms the reciprocating motion of the piston into rotary
motion.


# ⚙️ Bearings:

Bearings support the crankshaft and reduce friction during rotation.


# ⚙️ top plate:

top plate is the structural cover that protect valve , bearing cylinder housing under 
pressure.

# ⚙️ shaft:

it help to connect the crankshaft and flywheels.
