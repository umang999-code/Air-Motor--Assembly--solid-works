## 📌 Working Principle of Reciprocating Air Motor:

The air motor works by converting the energy of compressed air into
mechanical motion.

Compressed air is supplied to the cylinder. The pressure of the
air acts on the piston and produces a force.

This force causes the piston to move in a reciprocating manner.

The piston is connected to the connecting rod.

The connecting rod transfers the piston motion to the crank.

The crank is mounted on the crankshaft.

As the crank rotates, the reciprocating motion of the piston is
converted into rotary motion of the crankshaft.

The flywheel mounted on the crankshaft stores kinetic energy and
helps maintain smoother rotation.

# 🔬 Working Sequence:

## Step 1 – Air Enter

Compressed air enters the cylinder through the air inlet.

---

## Step 2 – Pressure on Piston

The compressed air applies pressure to the piston.

The resulting force causes the piston to move.

---

## Step 3 – Reciprocating Motion

The piston moves along the cylinder axis.

This produces linear reciprocating motion.

---

## Step 4 – Motion Transfer

The connecting rod transfers the piston movement to the crank.

---

## Step 5 – Motion Conversion

The crank converts the reciprocating motion into rotary motion.

---

## Step 6 – Crankshaft Rotation

The crankshaft rotates continuously as the piston mechanism operates.

---

## Step 7 – Flywheel Action

The flywheel stores rotational kinetic energy and helps maintain
more uniform rotation.

---

# ⚙️ Role of Major Components:

| Component | Function |

| Connecting Rod | Transfers piston motion |

| Crankshaft | Carries rotary output |
